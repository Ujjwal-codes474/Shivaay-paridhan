Our Story

From the Looms of Varanasi to Your Wardrobe

Shivaay Paridhan was born in 2026 from a deep love for India's rich weaving heritage. Founded in the lanes of Varanasi — the spiritual home of Banarasi silk — our journey began with a simple belief: every Indian woman deserves to wear art.

What started as a small curated collection has grown into a trusted brand, serving thousands of women across India. We work directly with master weavers, ensuring that every saree carries the warmth of human hands and the spirit of generations of craftsmanship.

We don't just sell sarees — we tell stories. Each thread, each motif, each border is a chapter of India's living textile tradition.

— Founder, Shivaay Paridhan

Purpose

Mission & Vision

Everything we do is driven by a commitment to Indian artistry and the women we serve.

🎯

Our Mission

To make authentic, handcrafted Indian sarees accessible to every woman — bridging the gap between traditional weavers and modern shoppers, while ensuring fair wages and sustainable practices across our supply chain.

🌟

Our Vision

To become India's most loved ethnic wear destination — a brand synonymous with trust, elegance, and cultural pride. We envision a world where handwoven Indian textiles are celebrated globally as the luxury they truly are.
